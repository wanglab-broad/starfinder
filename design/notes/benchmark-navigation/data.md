# Data

The inputs of a benchmark are the real sequencing inputs and the synthetic presets of the
[dataset catalog](../../../docs/datasets.md), described under [../wiki/dataset](../../../docs/wiki/dataset.md). The image
statistics define the measurements that compare real and synthetic images, and the
development calibration targets of the synthetic presets; they are recorded in
[image statistics and calibration targets](https://github.com/wanglab-broad/starfinder/blob/dev/design/notes/image-statistics.md).
