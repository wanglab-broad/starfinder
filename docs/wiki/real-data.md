# Real data

The real sequencing inputs are listed in the dataset catalog: their register IDs, local
versions, states, shapes, codebooks and limits
([Real sequencing inputs](../datasets.md#real-sequencing-inputs)), and the register
entries without a selected local input. The catalog also records the synthetic presets
and the historical synthetic and registration inputs. Availability in the catalog does
not qualify an input for an experiment; the benchmark register, kept outside the
repository, owns that decision.
